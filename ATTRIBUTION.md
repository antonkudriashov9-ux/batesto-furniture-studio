# Assets and provenance

This repository is an independent demonstration concept prepared for Batesto, not the workshop's official website. It does not accept orders or payments and does not automatically send form contents. Interior images are AI-generated concepts, not the workshop's completed projects.

- Batesto name, public business contacts and workshop banner: https://batesto.clients.site/ . The brand and workshop photograph remain their respective owner's materials; no ownership or blanket open-source licence is claimed for them.
- Manrope font: Google Fonts / SIL Open Font License. See `dist/assets/Manrope-OFL.txt`.
- Three.js and bundled addons: MIT. See `dist/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.
- PBR scans and HDR environment: Poly Haven and ambientCG, CC0. Individual sources and physical scales are recorded in `dist/assets/pbr/SOURCES.md`.
- CAD reference: original project geometry generated with CadQuery / OCCT. No manufacturer CAD assets are redistributed. See `cad/README.md` for capabilities and limitations.

A public repository makes the project inspectable; it does not transfer rights to the workshop's branding or photography. A real commercial launch requires the workshop's agreed content and business terms.
